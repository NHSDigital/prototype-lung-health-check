Prototype version 4.x

Last updated: 9 July 2026

Version 4.4

Added more support around the smoking history questions, including a short introduction before this section and a summary after each tobacco type.

More about version 4.4
  • Introduced a smoking history page to help people understand what they will be asked before they start this part of the questionnaire.
  • Added a summary at the end of each selected tobacco type so people can check those answers before moving on.
  • Separated smoking frequency and quantity questions to reduce the amount people need to answer on one page.
  • Tailored the smoking change questions so people are only asked frequency questions when there is more than one sensible option.
Open prototype

Version 4.3

Moved the tobacco smoking questions to the start of the service so people who are not eligible because of their smoking history find out earlier.

More about version 4.3
  • Asked people which types of tobacco they smoke or have smoked before asking other eligibility and health questions.
  • Asked whether people currently smoke one selected tobacco type, or which selected tobacco types they currently smoke if they chose more than one.
  • Used present or past tense tobacco questions based on whether someone currently smokes each selected tobacco type.
  • Moved the wider health questions to after the tobacco smoking section.
Open prototype

Version 4.2

Grouped related questions onto fewer pages and moved the smoking questions earlier in the journey, after the initial eligibility checks.

More about version 4.2
  • Combined related smoking history questions, such as when someone started, stopped and had breaks from smoking.
  • Combined tobacco frequency and quantity questions to reduce the number of pages in repeated tobacco sections.
  • Combined questions about smoking more or less over time into fewer pages.
  • Grouped related asbestos questions onto one page.
Open prototype

Version 4.1

Kept the version 4 journey but presented most questions one at a time so each page had a clearer focus.

More about version 4.1
  • Separated questions about height, weight, gender identity, sex at birth, ethnicity and education into individual steps.
  • Asked health and smoking history questions as individual pages before the check your answers page.
  • Kept tailored tobacco questions for different tobacco types, including cigarettes, rolling tobacco, cigars, cigarillos, pipes and shisha.
  • Included stop pages for people who had already completed the questionnaire by phone, were outside the age range, needed a face-to-face appointment, or were not eligible based on smoking history.
Open prototype

Version 4

Introduced the version 4 journey for checking whether someone may need a lung scan, including eligibility, health and detailed smoking history questions.

More about version 4
  • Started with checks for people who had already completed the questionnaire by phone, were outside the age range, or needed a face-to-face appointment.
  • Asked about height, weight, gender identity, sex at birth, ethnicity, education and wider health factors that may affect lung cancer risk.
  • Asked about different types of tobacco smoking and repeated relevant questions for each type selected.
  • Included a check your answers page before the confirmation page.
